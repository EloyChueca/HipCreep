## 1. Creep of conventional and cross-linked UHMWPE (dose and thermal treatment)

The radiation dose alone does not order the creep: the **thermal treatment after irradiation** (remelting above Tm vs
annealing below it) and the resulting crystallinity do.

### Laboratory

| Study | Material(s) | Finding on creep |
|---|---|---|
| Lewis & Carroll 2001, Biomed Mater Eng 11:167 | GUR 1050: control vs γ-crosslinked in air (low / high dose) | crosslinked creep compliance ×1.12–1.14 of control (compression) — used in HipCreep |
| Burger 2006 (PhD Pretoria); Burger & Weber 2004 (Orthop Proc) | γ 25 kGy air / N₂, annealed; 125 kGy (acetylene) | 25 kGy: 0.76–0.82 of virgin (2 h, RT); 125 kGy: 0.73 (RT), 0.55–0.63 at 50–60 °C — used |
| Estok et al. 2005, J Arthroplasty 20:239 (full text) | GUR 1020 γ 25–41 kGy N₂ vs e-beam 95 kGy + remelted (Durasul) | liners, 2 Hz, 4 M cycles: remelted HXLPE +10–17 % penetration — used |

**Consistency with HipCreep's grades**: annealed / remelted factor 0.85 / 1.13 = 0.75 vs Hamai 0.68 (same order);
remelted ≥ conventional (Estok +10–17 %; Glyn-Jones ≈ equal); vitamin-E < conventional (Takahashi 0.86). Clinical
early penetration also contains seating and run-in wear, so it is not used to calibrate.

## 2. Novelty check (points 1 and 2 of the method)

**Point 1 — minimum creep from the elevations (material above the original sphere, volume conservation).**
Not found. Closest work:
* Hernigou et al. 2008, Open Orthop J 2:51 (full text): flat unicompartmental tibial components; creep = CMM volume
  change − gravimetric wear (needs the original weight; not applicable to modular liners of unknown weight).
* Muratoglu et al. 2003, CORR 410:155: knee simulator, CMM scar volume vs gravimetric loss.
* Teeter et al. 2010 (J Arthroplasty 25:330; J Long Term Eff Med Implants 20:49): micro-CT deviation maps measure
  "wear plus creep" together; no separation.
* McDonald & Bloebaum 1995 (polarised light), Pezzotti 2011 / Miura 2015 / Tone 2020 (Raman): creep identified from
  the material structure, not from the geometry.
* Knee retrieval studies score "cold flow" qualitatively (Wasielewski 1994, Engh 1992, Cuckler 2003…).

**Point 2 — creep model + measured penetration + uncertainty, liner by liner.**
Not found. Separation has been done with: RSA time series (Sychterz 1999, Glyn-Jones 2008), Raman (Pezzotti, Miura,
Tone), gravimetry (Hernigou 2008), generic finite-element simulations (Bevill 2005, Penmetsa 2006, Liu 2012) or
laboratory creep tests (Estok 2005). None combines a creep model with recovery and Monte Carlo uncertainty with the 3D
penetration of each retrieved liner.
